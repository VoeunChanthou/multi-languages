Tip: Korean has no strong stress, so say every syllable evenly. The sound guide shows how words really sound when they join together, which can differ from the spelling (감사합니다 is said kahm-sah-hahm-nee-dah). Sentences ending in -요 are polite and safe to use with anyone. Items marked (casual) are only for close friends and younger people.
