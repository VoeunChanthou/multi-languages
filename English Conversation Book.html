ហ្វf: top teeth on the lower lip (five, coffee)
វv / w: v buzzes on the lip (very); w rounds the lips (water)

រ / លr: curl the tongue back without touching the roof (red); l: tongue behind the top teeth (light)
ឆ / ជch as in “chair”; j as in “job”

Tip: English words often end in sounds like s, t, d, z, l and v. Say these endings clearly, because they change the meaning: cat / cats, walk / walked. គន្លឹះ៖ ពាក្យអង់គ្លេសច្រើនបញ្ចប់ដោយសំឡេងព្យញ្ជនៈ។ ត្រូវបញ្ចេញសំឡេងចុងឲ្យច្បាស់ ព្រោះវាប្តូរអត្ថន័យ។ The Khmer letters are a close guide, not a perfect one, so tap the speaker and copy the voice.
